Minimalist AI Pro Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Minimalist AI Pro ("Minimalist Pro") is developed by Luo Hanjun. It runs compatible AI models on your own device. It is a separate app from Minimalist AI and does not read or migrate that app's chats, models, settings or purchases.

Local processing

Chat, image generation, file transcription and speech run on your device. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, remote logging, cloud AI model service or Minimalist account. Your prompts, chats, attachments, generated images, transcripts and generated audio are not sent to the developer.

What the app stores on your device

Inside its own app container the app stores: downloaded or imported models; chats and their attachments; separate drafts and settings for the chat, image and audio workspaces; custom backgrounds (a resized copy without location or other photo metadata) and appearance settings; image prompts, parameters, generated PNG files and image history; local copies of imported audio or video sound tracks, transcripts and their source file names; and generated WAV files with their voice, language, creation time and a short text preview.

Downloads and other network connections

The app connects to the internet only when you start one of these actions:

  • Model downloads. Recommended models are downloaded from the source shown in the app (currently Hugging Face, including Apple's Core ML Stable Diffusion repositories). If you turn on Settings > Downloads > Use China Mainland Download Mirror (on by default when the device's region or time zone is mainland China), Hugging Face downloads come from the same repositories on ModelScope (modelscope.cn) instead, except MiniCPM-V 4.7 packages, which have no mainland China source; a download link that ModelScope cannot serve keeps its original address. Recommended files are still checked against their pinned sizes and checksums. If you enter your own download address, the app connects to that public HTTPS address. Downloads contain model data only, never executable engine code.
  • Apple language resources. On-device transcription may ask the operating system to download Apple speech resources for a language.
  • Links you open, such as model source or license pages.

These hosts and network providers can process ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. The developer does not receive it.

Photos, files and microphone

You choose photos and files through Apple's system pickers, paste or drag and drop. The app does not access your microphone and does not record audio. It asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you choose to save a generated image there; it does not read your existing photos for that.

Local tools

If you turn on the JS Tool or Py Tool, programs written by the model run in isolated local runtimes on your device: a fresh WebKit worker for JavaScript and a bundled pocketpy interpreter for Python. They have no access to your files, network, other apps or app data, and nothing is uploaded.

Image generation safety

Generated images are always checked by a safety checker before they are shown or saved, and results it flags are discarded. The official Stable Diffusion 1.4 model is used under the CreativeML OpenRAIL-M license, which you accept before downloading it. If you import another Core ML model, you confirm that you may use it under its own license.

Contacting the developer

The app can prepare an email for an invitation, collaboration or problem report, addressed to LuoHanjun@u.nus.edu with a copy to Minimalist.AI@outlook.com. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself from Apple Mail or your email app. If you send it, the developer receives your name and email address as shown by your mail service, your message and any attachments you choose. The developer handles these personally to reply, investigate problems and discuss collaboration, and does not use them for advertising, profiling or AI training. Please do not include passwords or confidential material. Your email service handles drafts and delivery under its own settings.

Purchase

Minimalist AI Pro is a one-time paid download from the App Store. Apple handles payment and distribution. After installation the app does not need an online check to unlock its features.

Retention and deletion

You can delete chats, models and generated audio in the app. Image and transcript history currently has no in-app delete button; deleting the app removes it. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app normally removes its data, while offloading keeps it. On Mac, deleting the app may leave data behind. Device backups made by your Apple devices may include app data according to your settings; model and partial-download folders are excluded from backup unless you turn on Settings > Downloads > Include Models in Backups (iPhone and iPad), which adds finished models, not partial downloads, to backups and to the transfer to a new device. Copies you export or share are managed where you put them.

To ask for deletion of emails you sent to the developer, write to Minimalist.AI@outlook.com. Those copies will be deleted unless they must be kept under a collaboration agreement or applicable law.

Children

Minimalist AI Pro is a general-purpose tool and is not directed to children. AI output can be inaccurate or inappropriate; a parent or guardian should supervise use where appropriate.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date and published policy will be updated. Questions: Minimalist.AI@outlook.com.

Minimalist AI Pro 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日

Minimalist AI Pro（桌面名称 Minimalist Pro）由 Luo Hanjun 开发，在你自己的设备上运行兼容的 AI 模型。它是独立于 Minimalist AI 的另一个 App，不会读取或迁移原版的会话、模型、设置或购买记录。

本地处理

聊天、图片生成、文件转写和朗读都在你的设备上完成。App 没有广告、分析统计、追踪、远程日志、云端 AI 模型服务或账号系统。你的提示词、会话、附件、生成的图片、转写文本和生成的音频都不会发送给开发者。

设备上保存的内容

App 在自己的容器内保存：下载或导入的模型；会话及其附件；聊天、图片、声音三个工作区各自的草稿和设置；自定义背景（去除位置等照片元数据后缩放的副本）和外观设置；图片提示词、参数、生成的 PNG 和图片历史；导入的音频或视频音轨的本地副本、转写文本及其来源文件名；以及生成的 WAV 文件及其声音、语言、创建时间和一段文字预览。

下载和其他网络连接

App 只在你主动执行以下操作时联网：

  • 下载模型。推荐模型从 App 中标明的来源下载（目前为 Hugging Face，包括 Apple 的 Core ML Stable Diffusion 仓库）。如果你开启“设置 > 下载 > 使用中国大陆下载通道”（设备地区或时区为中国大陆时默认开启），来自 Hugging Face 的下载会改由魔搭社区（ModelScope，modelscope.cn）上的相同仓库提供，MiniCPM-V 4.7 模型包暂无国内来源，仍从 Hugging Face 下载；魔搭社区没有的下载链接仍使用原地址。推荐文件仍按固定的大小和校验值核对。如果你自己输入下载地址，App 会连接该公开 HTTPS 地址。下载的只是模型数据，不包含可执行的引擎代码。
  • Apple 语言资源。本地转写可能请求系统下载某种语言的 Apple 语音资源。
  • 你打开的链接，例如模型来源或许可页面。

这些网站和网络服务商可能按各自的隐私政策处理 IP 地址等一般连接信息，开发者不会收到这些信息。

照片、文件和麦克风

照片和文件由你通过 Apple 系统选择器、粘贴或拖放提供。App 不使用麦克风，也不录音。只有当你选择把生成的图片存入照片图库时，App 才会请求添加照片的权限，且不会因此读取你已有的照片。

本地工具

如果你开启 JS Tool 或 Py Tool，模型编写的程序会在设备上的隔离环境中运行：JavaScript 在全新的 WebKit Worker 中，Python 在内置的 pocketpy 解释器中。它们无法访问你的文件、网络、其他 App 或 App 数据，也不会上传任何内容。

图片生成安全

生成的图片在显示或保存前都会经过安全检查，被标记的结果会被丢弃。官方 Stable Diffusion 1.4 模型按 CreativeML OpenRAIL-M 许可使用，你在下载前需要同意该许可。如果你导入其他 Core ML 模型，需要确认你有权按其许可使用。

联系开发者

App 可以为邀请合作或报告问题准备一封邮件，收件人为 LuoHanjun@u.nus.edu，抄送 Minimalist.AI@outlook.com。只有你自己在 Apple 邮件或你的邮件 App 中发送后才会发出。发送后，开发者会收到邮件服务显示的你的姓名和邮箱、邮件内容以及你选择的附件。开发者本人处理这些邮件，仅用于回复、排查问题和沟通合作，不用于广告、画像或 AI 训练。请不要包含密码或机密内容。草稿和投递由你的邮件服务按其设置处理。

购买

Minimalist AI Pro 是在 App Store 一次性付费下载的 App，付款和分发由 Apple 处理。安装后无需联网验证即可使用全部功能。

保留与删除

你可以在 App 中删除会话、模型和生成的音频。图片和转写历史目前没有 App 内删除按钮，删除 App 会一并删除。在 iPhone 和 iPad 上删除 App 通常会删除其数据，卸载（Offload）则会保留。在 Mac 上删除 App 可能会留下数据。设备备份可能按你的设置包含 App 数据；模型和未完成下载的文件夹默认不会进入备份；如果你在 iPhone 或 iPad 上开启“设置 > 下载 > 备份和换机时包含模型”，已下载完成的模型（不含未完成的下载）会进入备份，并在换新设备时一起迁移。你导出或分享的副本由其所在位置管理。

如需删除你发给开发者的邮件，请写信至 Minimalist.AI@outlook.com。除非因合作协议或法律要求必须保留，这些副本将被删除。

儿童

Minimalist AI Pro 是通用工具，并非面向儿童。AI 输出可能不准确或不合适，必要时应由家长或监护人陪同使用。

更新与联系

政策变更时会更新生效日期和公开的政策。如有问题请联系 Minimalist.AI@outlook.com。
