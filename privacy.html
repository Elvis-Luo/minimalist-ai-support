Minimalist AI Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Minimalist AI is developed by Luo Hanjun and is designed to run compatible AI models on the user's device.

Local processing and optional services

The app contains no advertising, analytics, remote logging, cloud model API or Minimalist AI account system. Chat and model features do not upload your content to the developer. If you voluntarily email the developer, the developer receives the message and attachments you choose to send. Optional purchases and other external actions use the services described below.

On-device chat and model content

For chat and model features, chats, prompts, model output, selected files, attachment previews and extracted text, models, and settings are processed and stored locally in the app container. The app does not upload this chat or model content to the developer. Copies you choose to export or share, optional email attachments and system backups are handled separately by their corresponding services.

Optional model downloads

The app connects to the model source shown in the interface only when the user explicitly starts a recommended download. Current recommended files are hosted by Hugging Face. The host and network provider may process ordinary network information, such as IP address and request metadata, under their own policies. Minimalist AI does not receive that information from the host.

Photos and files

You choose which photos and files to provide through Apple's system pickers, paste, or drag and drop. Chat attachments are processed locally. The separate invitation form lets you choose photos and files to include in an email to the developer; these are not limited to local processing once you send them. The app does not automatically attach your chats, chat attachments or models to that email.

Optional one-time support

The six support options use Apple's StoreKit as voluntary, repeatable, consumable one-time purchases. They create no subscription and do not unlock, restrict or change any app feature. Apple handles payment; the app receives product and transaction information to show availability and purchase status. Apple's payment and App Store services handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Optional invitation and support email

The invitation form creates a separate draft addressed To LuoHanjun@u.nus.edu, with Cc LuoHanjunElvis@gmail.com. Selected attachments total at most 25 MiB. You confirm sending in Apple's mail composer or your chosen email app; Minimalist AI does not send the message automatically, operate its own sending server or process this draft with an AI model.

Email delivery, storage and draft synchronization use your email service. Saving a draft in Mail may synchronize its contents under that service's settings before you send it. A handoff to the mail system does not guarantee delivery. The mailto fallback does not carry attachments; you can export them and add them separately in your email app.

On the developer's side, Luo Hanjun personally handles invitation, collaboration and problem-report emails and their attachments. They are used only to reply, investigate reported problems and communicate about collaboration, not for advertising, user profiling or AI training. Please do not include passwords, model files, confidential documents or unrelated private chats.

Retention and deletion

You can delete models in Settings and chats in Local History. On iOS and iPadOS, Delete App normally removes local app data, while Offload App keeps documents and data. On Mac, deleting the application may leave data behind. Normal updates usually preserve local data but are not a substitute for backups.

System-managed device backups may include app data according to your Apple device settings. Downloadable model and partial-download directories are excluded from backup by the app. Files you export or share are handled by the destination you choose. Deleting the app or its data does not also delete exported or shared copies or existing backups; manage those separately at their destinations.

You can request deletion of correspondence and attachments held by the developer by emailing LuoHanjunElvis@gmail.com. The developer will delete those copies unless they must be retained under a collaboration agreement or applicable law. Deleting an app draft, chat or the app itself does not delete messages already sent, copies held by recipients, or drafts and backups held by email services. Those services manage their copies under their own settings and policies.

Children

Minimalist AI is a general-purpose tool and is not directed specifically to children. Model-generated content can be inaccurate or inappropriate; a parent or guardian should supervise use where appropriate.

This website

This static support site has no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or contact form. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary connection information under GitHub's privacy statement. Voluntary support emails are handled as described in the email and deletion sections above.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, its effective date will be updated. Questions or requests to delete your support correspondence can be sent to LuoHanjunElvis@gmail.com. See also the Support page.

Minimalist AI 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日

Minimalist AI 由 Luo Hanjun 开发，旨在用户设备上运行兼容的人工智能模型。

本地处理与可选服务

App 不包含广告、统计分析、远程日志、云端模型 API 或 Minimalist AI 账号系统。聊天和模型功能不会将内容上传给开发者。用户自愿给开发者发送邮件时，开发者会收到用户选择发送的正文和附件。可选购买及其他外部操作使用下文说明的相应服务。

本地聊天与模型内容

在聊天和模型功能中，聊天、提示词、模型输出、用户选择的文件、附件预览与提取文本、模型和设置在 App 容器中本地处理和保存。App 不会将这些聊天或模型内容上传给开发者。用户主动导出或分享的副本、可选邮件附件以及系统备份，由相应服务分别处理。

可选模型下载

只有用户主动开始推荐模型下载时，App 才连接界面标明的来源。目前推荐文件托管于 Hugging Face。文件托管方与网络服务商可能依其政策处理 IP 地址、请求信息等普通连接数据；开发者不会从托管方接收这些信息。

照片与文件

用户通过 Apple 系统选择器、粘贴或拖放自行选择提供的照片和文件。聊天附件在本地处理。独立的邀请表单允许用户选择照片和文件作为发给开发者的邮件附件；发送后，这些附件不再仅限于本地处理。App 不会自动把聊天、聊天附件或模型附在这封邮件中。

可选的一次性支持

六档支持选项通过 Apple StoreKit 提供自愿、可重复购买的消耗型一次性支持。它们不会产生订阅，也不会解锁、限制或改变任何 App 功能。付款由 Apple 处理；App 接收商品和交易信息，用于显示可购买状态和购买结果。Apple 的付款与 App Store 服务按照其自身条款和隐私政策处理信息。

可选的邀请与支持邮件

邀请表单建立独立草稿，收件人为 LuoHanjun@u.nus.edu，抄送 LuoHanjunElvis@gmail.com。所选附件合计最多 25 MiB。用户在 Apple 邮件编辑界面或自己选择的邮件 App 中确认发送；Minimalist AI 不会自动发送，不运营自己的发信服务器，也不会用 AI 模型处理这份草稿。

邮件的投递、存储和草稿同步由用户的邮件服务处理。在邮件 App 中保存草稿，可能在发送前按照该服务的设置同步其内容。交给邮件系统处理不代表保证送达。mailto 备用链接不携带附件；用户可以另行导出附件，再到邮件 App 中添加。

开发者一侧的邀请合作、问题报告邮件及其附件仅由 Luo Hanjun 本人处理，只用于回复、排查用户报告的问题和合作沟通，不用于广告、用户画像或 AI 训练。请勿提供密码、模型文件、机密文档或无关的私人聊天。

保留与删除

可在设置中删除模型，在本地历史中删除聊天。iOS 和 iPadOS 上的“删除 App”通常会移除本机 App 数据，而“卸载 App”会保留文稿与数据；Mac 上仅删除应用程序可能仍留下数据。正常覆盖更新通常保留本地数据，但不能代替备份。

系统管理的设备备份可能按照用户的 Apple 设备设置包含 App 数据；App 会将可重新下载的模型及下载断点目录排除在备份外。主动导出或分享的文件由用户选择的目标处理。删除 App 或 App 内数据不会同时删除已导出、已分享的副本或已有备份，请在相应位置分别管理。

如希望删除开发者持有的邮件及附件，可联系 LuoHanjunElvis@gmail.com。开发者会删除这些副本，但合作合同或适用法律要求保留的情况除外。删除 App 中的草稿、聊天或 App 本身，不会删除已经发送的邮件、收件人持有的副本，或邮件服务保存的草稿与备份；相应服务会按照自身设置和政策管理其副本。

儿童

本 App 为通用工具，并非专门面向儿童。模型生成内容可能不准确或不适宜，监护人应在适当情况下进行指导。

本网站

本静态网站没有广告、分析脚本、跟踪 Cookie 或联系表单。GitHub Pages 作为托管服务商可能按照其隐私声明处理普通连接信息。用户主动发送的支持邮件，按照上文邮件与删除部分的说明处理。

更新与联系

政策变更时会更新生效日期。如有问题或希望删除支持邮件记录，请联系 LuoHanjunElvis@gmail.com。
