Minimalist AI Support

Minimalist AI runs compatible language and vision models locally on iPhone, iPad, and Apple-silicon Mac.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm that the model is fully downloaded or imported and selected.
  • For vision, confirm that the main model and matching projector are paired.
  • Make sure enough storage is available for the download and temporary verification file.
  • Try the smallest recommended model for the device's memory tier.
  • Record the app version, device and OS, model filename, and exact error.

Privacy when reporting a problem

Do not send private chats, confidential documents, model files, passwords, or API keys. Non-sensitive reproduction steps and an error screenshot are usually sufficient.

Contact

Email LuoHanjunElvis@gmail.com. Support is available in English or Chinese. No support account is required.

Privacy Policy

Minimalist AI 使用支持

Minimalist AI 可在 iPhone、iPad 和 Apple 芯片 Mac 上本地运行兼容的语言与视觉模型。

联系前可先检查

  • 确认模型已经完整下载或导入，并已在设置中选择。
  • 使用视觉功能时，确认主模型与对应的视觉投影文件匹配。
  • 确认设备存储足够容纳下载文件及临时校验文件。
  • 如果设备内存不足，可尝试符合设备内存级别的较小推荐模型。
  • 记录 App 版本、设备与系统版本、模型文件名及完整错误提示。

保护隐私

请勿发送私人聊天、机密文档、模型文件、密码或 API 密钥。通常只需不含敏感信息的复现步骤和错误截图。

联系方式

邮箱：LuoHanjunElvis@gmail.com。支持中文和英文，无需注册支持账号。
